River landforms explained 🗻

Click. Discover. Enjoy. Six shapes that flowing water creates.

Waterfall

Water drops over a hard rock ledge. Softer rock below wears away faster, deepening the plunge pool.

Gorge

A narrow, steep valley carved by a fast river cutting downward through rock.

Confluence

The point where two rivers meet and join. The smaller river is a tributary.

Meander

A looping bend that forms as the river erodes the outer bank and deposits on the inner bank.

Oxbow lake

A curved lake left behind when a meander loop is cut off from the main river.

Delta

A fan of channels and fertile land formed where a river drops its sediment at the sea.

Rivers of India 🇮🇳

Indian rivers are often grouped into two families.

🏔️

Himalayan rivers

Fed by glaciers and rain, so they flow all year. Examples: Ganga, Yamuna, Brahmaputra and Indus. They carve deep valleys and build wide plains.

🌾

Peninsular rivers

Rainfall-fed and more seasonal. Examples: Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri and Narmada. Many flow through plateaus and form deltas on the east coast.

Gangotri Glacier

The Bhagirathi begins near Gaumukh in the Himalayas.

Devprayag

Bhagirathi and Alaknanda meet here, and the river is known as the Ganga from this confluence.

Northern Plains

The river slows, widens and deposits fertile silt over the plains.

Sundarbans Delta

Together with the Brahmaputra, it forms one of the world’s largest deltas before reaching the Bay of Bengal.

The water cycle: how rivers are refilled ☁️

Everything organized in five simple steps.

1. Evaporation

Sun warms oceans, lakes and rivers, turning water into vapour.

2. Condensation

Vapour rises, cools and forms clouds.

3. Precipitation

Rain and snow fall, especially over mountains.

4. Runoff

Water flows downhill and through the ground into streams.

5. Collection

Streams join rivers, rivers reach the sea, and the cycle begins again.

Life in and around rivers 🐟

Rivers support many plants, animals and people.

🐬

River dolphins

The Ganges river dolphin is found in the Ganga–Brahmaputra basin and is an important sign of river health.

🐟

Fish & insects

Clean, oxygen-rich water supports fish and insects that feed birds and other wildlife.

🦆

Birds

Wetlands along rivers give ducks, storks and kingfishers places to feed and nest.

🌾

People & farms

Rivers provide drinking water, irrigation, fishing and transport for millions of people.

Your Next Big Upgrade Awaits: river vocabulary 📖

Learn these words and every map and documentary becomes easier to read.

Source

The place where a river begins, such as a spring or glacier.

Tributary

A smaller stream that flows into a bigger river.

Confluence

The meeting point of two rivers.

Watershed

The land area that drains into a river system.

Floodplain

Flat land beside a river that floods and receives fertile silt.

Estuary

The wide mouth of a river where fresh water meets the sea.

Erosion

Wearing away of rock and soil by water, wind or ice.

Sediment

Sand, silt and clay carried and deposited by water.
